Health Score (Rule-Based Demo Logic)

In JS, compute score like:

healthScore =
  (batteryHealth * 0.4) +
  (solarScore * 0.3) +
  (generatorScore * 0.3);

Where:

batteryHealth = battery %

solarScore = 100 if solar ON else 40

generatorScore penalizes bad behavior

Status Mapping
if (healthScore >= 80) → Healthy
if (healthScore >= 60) → At Risk
else → Critical
